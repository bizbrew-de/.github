# BizBrew.de

**Custom software and digital product development for businesses.**

[BizBrew.de](https://bizbrew.de) designs, develops, deploys, and maintains tailored digital solutions based on each client's goals, brand, and operational requirements.

## Services

- Business websites and web applications
- Flutter, Android, and iOS applications
- Backend systems, APIs, and databases
- Dashboards, portals, and internal business tools
- Third-party integrations and workflow automation
- Cloud deployment, monitoring, and maintenance
- Technical consulting and long-term support

## How we work

1. Understand the business and define requirements.
2. Plan the content, user experience, architecture, and delivery scope.
3. Design and develop the solution.
4. Test functionality, security, accessibility, and performance.
5. Obtain approval before production deployment.
6. Maintain and improve the delivered product.

## Repository standards

Client projects and shared services remain separated. Each repository should document its setup, environment variables, test commands, deployment process, ownership, and maintenance requirements. Secrets and client-confidential information must never be committed.

Most repositories are private because they contain proprietary or client-owned work.
